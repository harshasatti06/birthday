FOR MY SISTER 18.10.2005

Dear Chelli,

Happy Birthday to someone who was never technically my sister, but somehow became one of the most important people in my life.

We may not share the same blood, the same childhood or the same family name. But honestly, I don't think that's what makes someone family.

I don't know what the future will look like. We will grow older. Life will become busier. We may have different paths, different responsibilities, and different places to be. But I hope one thing never changes...

I hope we always remain the kind of people who can find our way back to each other.

On your birthday, I don't just wish you a happy day. I wish you a beautiful life. I wish you people who genuinely appreciate your heart. I wish you success in everything you work for. I wish you peace when life feels difficult. And I wish you countless reasons to smile.

Never forget how special you are.

Thank you for being you. And thank you for becoming my sister without ever needing the same blood.

Happy Birthday, Chelli 🤎

Always cheering for you,
Annaya
